Before we use AI on your resume

Import, ATS check, job recommendations and bullet rewriting send the text of your resume (name, contact details, work history, skills) to third-party AI providers so they can process it. Your photo is not sent. AI output can be inaccurate.

Details are in our Privacy Notice & Terms. Everything else on this page stays in your browser.
